## Claim

(1) **Gamma_red = N**, with the isomorphism Theta: N -> Gamma_red, phi -> hat(phi-bar) (phi not in A), alpha phi -> hat(alpha-bar phi-bar), alpha^* -> alpha^*. It is an isomorphism of dg algebras. Hypotheses used: Thm 1.1's (non-negative grading, no degree-0 loop at i), the paper's printed presentation of Gamma (W1), and d^2 = 0 on M (J = (X, dX) a dg ideal). **Acyclicity of D(i) and finite dimension are not used by the argument** (nor is J = 0), so this link of the chain End(T) = H_0(Gamma) = H_0(Gamma_red) = H_0(N) = H_0(kM) is closed relative to those inputs, at the level of rigour of B of E-214, and does not by itself separate E-211 from E-199.
(2) Hence End(T) = H_0(kM) now rests on: Thm 7.5 (printed, complete), the paper's presentation of Gamma, the steps marked WEAK below, and Evidence B of E-214. Nothing here touches H_1(kM) or J.
(3) It does NOT say T is tilting. Side finding (hand, below): A_3/rad^2, i = 2 gives H_0(kM) = k[3 -> 2] x k with H_1(kM) != 0, i.e. a silting, non-tilting mutation; Coxeter polynomial of that H_0 is not that of A_3/rad^2.
Refutation: a reduced basic path p with hat p not in the span of the Theta-images, or a V-word whose Theta-image is not hat of the barred path.

## Evidence (proof)

Notation: composition right to left (as in the .tex); V = {phi (phi not in A), alpha phi, alpha^*}, X as above; kM is the free path algebra on V + X (Steps 1-4).

**Step 1 (N is T(V)/(rho)).** kM/(X) = T(V). J := (X, dX) = (X, rho) with rho_x := dx mod (X). Computing from Def. 3.2: for x = phi alpha^-1, the term (d phi) alpha^-1 is in (X), and dec(...) differs from the plain path by Delta = 1 - sum alpha^-1 alpha, whose alpha^-1 part is in (X). So rho_x = (-1)^{||phi||} phi alpha^* - (-1)^{chi(t phi)} (d phi)/alpha with (d phi)/alpha read as the plain path word in V (a path of Q through i is cut into V-letters: an arrow into i followed by an A-arrow is the letter alpha phi'', an arrow into i followed by a non-A arrow is two letters). Likewise for alpha phi beta^-1. No alpha^* occurs in the tail of rho (E-214 point 3: for t(phi) = i the sum_b b^* (b phi)/alpha term is killed by the same ideal). Hand-check of (d phi)/alpha: p = p' alpha with p' starting at t(alpha) != i (alpha is not a loop), so no A-letter is ever the first letter of p'. OK.

**Step 2 (basis of N).** Order V-words by (number of alpha^* letters, then length-lex). Each rho has leading word phi alpha^* (or (alpha phi) beta^*): a letter out of i then alpha^*. Tails have fewer alpha^* letters, so rewriting terminates, and by E-214 C there are no overlaps (alpha^* is never a left letter). So N has basis the V-words in which alpha^* occurs only as the leftmost letter (every V-letter leaving i is a phi out of i or alpha phi'' with phi'' a loop at i, and none may sit left of alpha^*). No dg input needed here; this is E-214's C but only the algebra half, which is a diamond-lemma statement and complete.

**Step 3 (basis of Gamma_red).** Gamma has basis the basic paths in Q-bar (paper). A Q-bar path is a Q-word in barred letters (psi-bar phi-bar = psi E M phi is a product of two arrows, no further relation). Also hat(p' alpha_*) = 0: with |alpha_*| = 0, d(p' alpha_*) = (dp') alpha_* - (-1)^{|p'|} p' alpha-bar, and (dp' alpha_*)/beta-bar = 0 while (-p' alpha-bar)(-1)^{|p'|}/beta-bar = -(-1)^{|p'|} p' delta, so hat(p'alpha_*) = p'alpha_* - p'alpha_* = 0. So Gamma_red is spanned by hat p for p reduced basic with no alpha_*; these are the paths [alpha^*] bar(w), w a Q-word not ending (rightmost) in an A-arrow. They are linearly independent because hat p = p + (terms ending in alpha_*).
Bijection: normal V-words <-> such p, by cutting w at passages through i (arrow in then A-arrow out = letter alpha phi''). Same graded dimension in every bidegree (vertex pair, degree): Theta is letter-by-letter, so degrees add on both sides and it suffices to match letters. phi (not in A): ||phi|| = |phi| - chi(s) + chi(t) = |phi-bar| (this covers t(phi) = i and loops: M phi, M phi E). alpha phi (t(phi) = i): ||alpha phi|| = ||phi|| - 1 = |phi| - chi(s phi) + 1 - 1, and |alpha-bar phi-bar| = -1 + (|phi| - chi(s phi) + 1), equal. alpha^*: 0 = 0. Vertex pairs agree by construction (alpha^*: tail(alpha) -> i; alpha phi: s(phi) -> t(alpha)). Basis match is also run by script on 8 small cases (dimensions only).

**Step 4 (Theta is an algebra map, kills rho).** hat is multiplicative in the sense hat p hat q = hat(hat p . q) (paper, l. ~1000) and hat p . q = p q unless q begins (leftmost) with alpha^*. That exception never fires for normal words: alpha^* occurs only as the leftmost letter of a normal word (Step 2), so no proper right factor q begins with it; and q = alpha^* itself is the case computed as the printed relation. So for a V-word w without alpha^*, Theta(w) = hat(bar w). For a word phi alpha^* the product gives the printed relation (l. 1014-1021 in the paper); its value is exactly -(-1)^{|phi|} hat((d phi)/alpha) = Theta of the tail of rho (sign: (-1)^{||phi||}, ||phi|| = |phi| - 1 + chi, matches E-202). So Theta factors through N, and Theta is bijective on the bases of Steps 2-3 (normal words go to hat(bar w), the basis).

**Step 5 (Theta commutes with d).** On generators: d(hat phi) = hat(red(d phi-bar)) (Lemma 7.4), with d phi-bar = \overline{d phi} (t phi != i) or -\overline{d phi} + sum_b b^* \overline{b phi} (t phi = i). Compare Theta(partial phi) = Theta(dec red d phi) [+ sum b^* b phi]. Mod J, dec = plain, Theta(plain word) = hat(bar word) by Step 4, red commutes with bar (a path starting with alpha-bar corresponds to one of Q starting with alpha), and the term b^* \overline{b phi} is a reduced basic path, so hat of it is b^* hat(b phi). Same for alpha phi: d(alpha-bar phi-bar) = (-1)^{|alpha-bar|} alpha-bar d phi-bar = + \overline{alpha d phi} (the extra term alpha-bar beta^* from d phi-bar = ... + sum_b b^* \overline{b phi} vanishes, since alpha-bar beta^* = 0: phi alpha^* = 0 for phi != alpha_* in Gamma), matching partial(alpha phi) = dec(alpha red d phi) (sign +, OK; I first got a minus sign by forgetting |alpha-bar| = -1). alpha^*: both 0. Both sides satisfy Leibniz and Theta is an algebra map, so Theta is a chain map; J is a dg ideal (d^2 = 0, Sect. 8), so the quotient differential is well defined. Hence Theta: N -> Gamma_red is a dg isomorphism.

**WEAK steps (what would break it).**
(W1) The paper's presentation of Gamma (basic paths a basis, arrows phi-bar, alpha^*, alpha_*) is used, not checked; Step 3 depends on it, including that Q-bar paths = Q-words (no collapse of E M = 1 beyond the printed relations).
(W2) hat p hat q = hat(hat p q) is printed with a two-line proof; I used it for q containing a loop at i with both bars (M phi E); not re-derived. I did not check the bimodule bookkeeping for parallel arrows beyond Step 1's observation.
(W3) "reduced" in the paper means "does not start with alpha-bar"; paths starting with alpha_* are reduced but hat kills them (Step 3), so the paper's "Gamma/Gamma_red is generated by reduced paths starting with alpha_* or alpha-bar" is slightly off, harmless.

## Hand example, X non-empty (A_3/rad^2, i = 1; added in response)

Q: phi: 1->2 (deg 0), psi: 2->3 (deg 0), omega: 1->3 (deg 1), d omega = psi phi. i = 1, A = {phi} (degree-0 arrows STARTING at i), no arrow enters 1. M: omega (||omega|| = 1 - 1 + 0 = 0), psi (0), phi^* : 2->1 (0), and the anti-composition omega phi^{-1}: 2->3 (degree ||omega|| + 1 = 1) = X. partial(omega phi^{-1}) = omega phi^* - psi (paper's Example, l. 403-436ff.; here (-1)^{||omega||} = 1, -(-1)^{chi(t omega)} = -1, d omega / phi = psi).
- N = T(omega, psi, phi^*)/(omega phi^* - psi): normal words e1, e2, e3, omega, psi, phi^* (omega phi^* is rewritten to psi; nothing leaves 3). dim 6, all degree 0, degree 1 empty (omega phi^{-1} was in X).
- Gamma (all basic paths of Qbar, arrows phi-bar = phi E (deg -1), omega-bar = omega E (0), psi (0), phi^* (0), phi_* (0)): e1, e2, e3, phi-bar, omega-bar, psi, phi^*, phi_*, psi phi-bar, phi^* phi-bar, phi^* phi_*, psi phi_*: degree -1: 3, degree 0: 9. The script's count of the paths without alpha_* (9 = 3 in degree -1, 6 in degree 0) matches the 9 of these 12 that contain no alpha_*. Reduced and hat-surviving: e1, e2, e3, omega-bar, psi, phi^* (hat kills phi^* phi_* and psi phi_*: e.g. hat(psi phi_*) = psi phi_* + (-1)^0 (d(psi phi_*))/phi-bar . phi_* = psi phi_* - psi phi_* = 0). So Gamma_red has dim 6, degree 0.
- Theta: omega -> hat(omega-bar) = omega-bar + psi phi_* (d omega-bar = psi phi-bar, so the correction is +psi phi_*), psi -> psi, phi^* -> phi^*. Theta(omega phi^*) = (omega-bar + psi phi_*) phi^* = psi phi_* phi^* = psi = Theta(psi): the relation rho is killed, as claimed. Theta is a bijection on the 6 basis elements (dimension 6 = 6, triangular: hat omega-bar = omega-bar + lower).
- d: on N all d vanish (d omega = psi phi has phi in A, so red kills it); on Gamma_red d(omega-bar + psi phi_*) = psi phi-bar + psi (-phi-bar) = 0. Agree. H_0(N) = N = k[2 -> 1 -> 3] (psi = omega phi^*), the APR tilt, dim 6, H_1 = 0.
So the claim is tested here where X is non-empty and the quotient by J is non-trivial (without X, kM has the extra degree-1 generator and words with phi^* inside). One example only; the same-construction caveat of item 4 applies to the script.

M: phi (||phi|| = 1), psi phi (1->3, degree 0), psi^* (3->2, degree 0), omega (degree 1); X empty, so N = kM = Gamma_red. partial phi = psi^*(psi phi), partial omega = psi phi (no Delta: path enters i and leaves by an A-arrow).
