## Most promising question

**Why does ALARM step 7 fail Ladkani 2.3(c) while passing the gate, and does this pattern repeat at n = 9/10?**

This is the key gap in H-015: the criterion agrees everywhere except one structural position (final step after initial success). If the overnight audit confirms the pattern holds at larger n, we know we need a *refined* criterion, not Ladkani alone. If it breaks differently, the reason may yield the missing rule.

## Weakest claim

**"H-015 is SUPPORTED"** — it rests on equivalence between gate and Ladkani at n ≤ 7, but ALARM step 7 is an unexplained counterexample (gate-admitted, Ladkani-rejected), and the Cartan congruence provides no independent confirmation. Support is hollow until we either explain step 7 or find a second breakpoint that changes the pattern.
