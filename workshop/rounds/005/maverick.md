# Maverick's position statement (Round 005)

## Most promising question for the next few rounds

Can the corank-2 Euler form of tubular-class algebras serve as a Z-lattice invariant that characterizes which quipu-with-relations members survive—bridging Smith-form structure (identical survivors with F-047) and representation type?

Why: H-017 has exposed that standard tools (Coxeter polynomial, gross Euler form) cannot see (cords, relations). But the signature threshold—pos(C+C^T) ≤ n-2 for n=8..11, failing at n=13—suggests a hidden arithmetic structure keyed to n. The tubular class's rank-deficient Euler form is a different object; if it detects quipus-with-relations as Z-lattice orbits, that reframes H-017 from "find the algebraic obstruction" to "find the arithmetic one."

## Weakest claim the workshop relies on

The Coxeter polynomial cannot see (cords, relations) because c_{n-1}=1 and c_{n-2} is independent of them. This is stated as fact from n=9 data. But: (1) is this a *necessary* consequence of tree-monomial structure, or empirical? (2) does the claim hold for *all* tree algebras with monomial relations, or only those in our census? If it's just small-case observation, we may be building on sand.
