## Claim

The failing step of seed 1614 is not in `isMutable`, `mutateAtVertex` or `reduce`. The round-118 harness (`skeptic_conv3.repo_input`) passes the relation coefficient -1 as the residue 32002 = 32003 - 1. The repo computes over the rationals, where `x + 32002 y` and `y + 32002 x` (the length-2 relation and its length-3 consequence, whose scalings differ) are independent and together force x = y = 0. So the "redundant" relation is not redundant for the repo, the repo is handed a different algebra (paths 0>1>3>4 and 0>2>3>4 are zero), and its gate and children rightly disagree with the tilting verdict computed for the intended algebra. With coefficient -1 the repo agrees with Hom-verified tilting at all 8 of 8 vertex/side cases of seed 1614 (gate and child), with the redundant relation present or deleted. Over the whole regenerated N sample (936 parents, n = 5, 6) the failures fall from 71 to 8; the 8 are all `gate True, tilting False` on minimal length-3 presentations (E-080's kind, unchanged). No "nomatch" (wrong child) remains in the sample. Consequence: H-030's sufficient condition ("a redundant length-3 relation breaks the repo") is false as stated; what remains is the E-080 over-admission. It does not claim the repo is right for rational coefficients other than +-1, or for n >= 7.

## Evidence

Deleting relation 2 flips every extension to nonzero, which is the "0 of 8 after deletion" of E-310; that was the same coefficient change, not minimality.

Decision, refuse or minimalise: neither. The repo already treats a redundant relation correctly (`isInIdeal` is by ideal, `reduce` calls `_minimalGenerators`, test 2 below pins the redundancy).
