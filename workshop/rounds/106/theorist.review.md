## True?

I found no error in what was computed. Gaps, none of them fatal:

- Control C1 is weak evidence of power. It flips a sign, so I stops being closed (399 non-closed cells). H(I) is then defined only through a formula that assumes closure. The C1 count of 34 is not a clean homology. The "NO" there mostly says "not a dg ideal". C2 is the real homology control (closed, H != 0 in 7 cells), but it is a different ideal, not a near-miss of I. Neither tests the case that matters: a closed ideal of the same shape as I that fails to cancel.
- All six models have loops of degree 1 or 2 with d(phi) in span of short paths through alpha, beta. M3 and M3b are the only ones where the loop is not the only degree-1 generator at i. There is no model with two loops at i, a loop of degree 3, or d(phi) with a term not passing through an A-arrow. These are the cases where unitriangularity could break.
- The claim "Lemma A's premise is false on these models" rests on one hand-computed d(f a^-1) in M1 (Evidence 2). Not re-derived here. It is consistent with the E-279 statement, so I did not chase it.
- The sign set 'last' = -1 (Theorist's own question): the script uses `{'last': -1}`. d^2 = 0 passes on all models, which constrains signs but does not prove they are the printed ones. Since H(I) = 0 survived M2 (d f = 0, signs of d(phi) terms irrelevant), the result is not obviously sign-sensitive. I did not test the other sign set.
