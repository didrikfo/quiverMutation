## Claim

Let M be Oppermann's mutated dg quiver (Def. 3.2, arXiv:1504.02617v1) of a parent with a loop phi of degree m >= 1 at i. Let I be the dg ideal of kM generated by the Theta letters (phi alpha^-1, alpha phi beta^-1) and their differentials; N = kM/I is the algebra of Prop. 7.6 (generators phi, alpha phi, alpha^*; relations phi alpha^* = -+ (d phi)/alpha, ...). Then H(I) = 0, i.e. kM -> N is a quasi-isomorphism, in every computed cell (weight <= 12 for M1-M5, M3b; <= 9 for M7, M7b, M8A, M8B) of each of the ten loop models below. Answer to the T5 open point: YES, on these ten models, partial in scope. It is a computation, not a proof of the loop filtration, and it does not say how Lemma A's argument is to be repaired (Lemma A's premise, d_1 theta = monomial, is false on these models: d(phi alpha^-1) contains the generator b = (d phi)/alpha, see Evidence 2). A model with H(I) != 0 and a closed d would refute it; none found.

## Evidence

- M1: loop f deg 1, d f = (a,b). This is the Construction-2.2 model of kQ/(a then b), global dimension 2, Ext^2(S_i,S_i) = 1 (the E-211 situation, cyclic D(i)).
- M2: same quiver, d f = 0.  M4: two A-arrows a1, a2: i -> j, d f = (a1,b) - (a2,b) (6 Theta letters).

1. Method. Weights: w(a^*) = -w(a), w(a phi) = w(phi)+w(a), w(phi b^-1) = w(phi)-w(b), so d is homogeneous (checked on every letter). Cells (source, target, weight, degree) are finite because every cycle has positive weight; words are enumerated with a prefix-weight bound. I is spanned by words containing a Theta letter and by u (d theta) v; H_d(I) = dim I_d - rk(d|I_d) - rk(d|I_{d+1}) by exact elimination. d^2 = 0 and closure of I are asserted per model. The differential is `scholar_oppermann_sign.py` (Def. 3.2 as printed, sign set that passes E-202); a loop of degree 1 passes its d^2 test (E-202 sample), the degree-2 models M3, M3b pass d^2 = 0 here.
2. Why Lemma A's route does not cover this. In M1, d(f a^-1) = (a f a^-1)a^* - a^* f + b (signs as coded): the term b is a bare generator, so theta cancels a generator, not a monomial xy (the E-279 point). The homology is nonetheless 0, so the statement survives; the proof shape must be a unitriangular elimination (E-273 (3)).
3. Reading. The a^* f term and the (alpha f)(f alpha^-1)-type terms of d(g alpha^-1) in M3 (junction at i) also cancel. M4/M5 (several A-arrows, so several (phi, alpha, beta) triples) behave the same.
4. What could not be built. A degree-0 loop l in A (the case excluded by Thm 1.1) makes l^* a loop at i of weight -w(l): words (l^*)^k have unbounded negative weight, every cell is infinite, so the truncated H is meaningless. No planted loop of the excluded kind was therefore run; the NO controls are C2 and N2 (C1 only checks closure).
5. Side note. An earlier draft model (d g = f f for a loop g of degree 2) had d^2 != 0 under the coded Def. 3.2; the cause was my model (|d g| = 2, wrong degree), not the formula. Nothing to report there.
