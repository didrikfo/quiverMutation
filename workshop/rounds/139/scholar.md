## Claim

(1) **Scherotzke, arXiv:0906.4987v1, Theorem 5.7** (source l. 1145; number matched in
the compiled PDF): *"Let A := kA_n/I with n ≥ 4 and I is generated by the path of length
n−1. Then the Auslander-Reiten quiver of D^b(A) is isomorphic to Z[D_n]. If n is even,
then [−1] acts as the identity on D_n. If n is odd [−1] acts as the involution on D_n.
Furthermore A is derived equivalent to kD_n."* Her A_n has n vertices (Example 5.5:
1→2→3→4), so I is the one longest path and A = Λ(n,n-1) = N_n(n-1), the record's
`A_n` of E-328. Field: "a field k" (l. 113); the setting of BGS, used below, not checked. With Fosse 2305.06642 Cor. 4.6 (B_n ~ A_n,
matched in source, E-280/E-285) this gives F-056's whole secondary input,
