## Claim

1. **H-024 (necessity step): supported by** Ladkani, *On derived equivalences of categories of sheaves over finite posets*, arXiv:math/0610685: Cor. 3.13 (derived equivalent => zeta matrices 1_X, 1_Y Z-congruent via [F]: K_0 -> K_0; the Euler matrix 1_X^{-1} appears in the proof, [F]^t 1_Y^{-1} [F] = 1_X^{-1}, and inverses of congruent matrices are congruent, which is equivalent), Lemma 3.14 and Cor. 3.15 (M M^{-t} conjugated by the same P). Numbers are by counting `\newtheorem[section]` environments in the LaTeX (no `\label`; Prop. 3.11 = Euler form of simples = Moebius, Def. 3.12 congruent, Cor. 3.13, Lemma 3.14, Cor. 3.15). **Caveat:** the statements are for incidence algebras of posets. The proof uses only "a triangle equivalence induces an isometry of K_0 for the Euler form" and <[P_x],[S_y]> = delta_xy, so it holds for any finite-dimensional algebra of finite global dimension, LNAs included (acyclic quiver); this extension is ours, not a stated theorem. One-sentence proof for any finite-dimensional algebra A of finite global dimension: a derived equivalence F induces an isometry [F] of K_0 = Z^n with the Euler form <[P_x],[S_y]> = delta_xy, whose Gram matrix in the projective basis is C^{-1} (C the Cartan matrix, the form is unimodular since gldim < infinity), so C_B^{-1} = [F]^t C_A^{-1} [F] and hence C_B = [F]^{-1} C_A [F]^{-t}, Z-congruence. The text notes "if M1, M2 are congruent so are M1^{-1}, M2^{-1}", which is the passage from Euler matrix to Cartan matrix C. The simultaneous P with P Phi_A = Phi_B P is the proof of Lemma 3.14 (Phi is, up to sign, M M^{-t}, conjugated by the same P), not its statement.
2. **Weaker, independent statement: supported by** Bessenrodt-Holm, arXiv:math/0504547, Theorem 2.2 (first theorem of section 2, after Example 2.1 by count; label `unimod`): for any finite-dimensional algebra the unimodular *equivalence* class of C_A (invariant factors, det) is derived invariant; the paper attributes it to Bocian-Skowronski, J. reine angew. Math. 580 (2005), proof of Prop. 1.5 (UNVERIFIED, not fetched). This needs no finite global dimension but is weaker than congruence.
3. **Nikulin 1.13.3 / 1.14.2: not addressed** (no new source). E-269 does not use it; E-265's 7 q_S NOs do not use it either (q_S differing is enough for non-congruence). Only the "same genus => S isometric" upgrade would.
4. **Independent invariants (a): contradicted as candidates.** Hochschild cohomology: HH^*(A) = k for every LNA. This is F-019/idea 22's derivation, not a statement in 2312.14699; I re-checked its two inputs in the LaTeX: Bardzell resolution (label `bardzell_resn`, on-file number Thm 3.6; numbers unrecomputable, no LaTeX engine, shared `[equation]` counter) and the cochain basis p||b with p, b parallel (section 5, `\parallel{p}{b}`, s(p) = s(b), t(p) = t(b)); on a linear A_n only one path joins two vertices, and p in Gamma_n, n >= 1, lies in I, so the complex stops at degree 1. Avella-Alaminos-Geiss: **not addressed** (gentle only); F-019 shows gentle LNAs are all in the A_n class (not rechecked). Bessenrodt-Holm q-Cartan det (label `q-det`, gentle quivers, number not recomputed; formula prod (1-(-q)^k)^{c_k}): empty on LNAs, no oriented cycles gives det C_A(q) = 1 (unitriangular), the on-file conclusion, re-derived. So among the invariants reviewed (HH^*, q-Cartan det, unimodular class) there is no independent second NO on LNAs; AAG, derived Picard/Serre data and non-gentle cases are not addressed, and the claim is no wider.
5. **What is a second NO already**: the 8 pairs of E-259 are certified inequivalent by the quipu theorem (2305.06642 via `quipuForAlmostSeparateLNA`), which is independent of Cartan data. The Cartan NOs therefore agree with an independent certificate on all 8; they are the only NOs on pairs without a quipu name that rest on H-024.
6. **(b) positive controls: supported by** Ueda, arXiv:2302.02880, Cor. 1.3 (= Cor. 5.15, label `Main 2`; intro numbering by count: Thm 1.1, 1.2, Cor 1.3, Thm 1.4, 1.5, Cor 1.6) and Cor. 1.6 (= Cor. 5.17, label `Main 4`); Ladkani, arXiv:0911.5137, Cor. 1.2 (the `c:rectline` corollary, numbered 1.2 by count and cited as [Cor 1.2] by Ueda): A(mn, m+1) ~ A(mn, n+1) ~ kA_m (x) kA_n; A(n,l) = KA_n / rad^l, digit string "l"*(n-l)+"0"*(l-2).
