## Claim

Setting and notation are those of E-319: kM is Def. 3.2 at i, A = the degree-0 arrows out of i, Theta = letters with a suffix, I = (Theta, d Theta).

**Proposition (LF) (proved).** Let the parent be a Construction-2.2 model of a basic finite-dimensional algebra (so d p != 0 for every letter of degree >= 1, and no word of d p is a single arrow), with no degree-0 loop at i. Give it positive letter weights w that make d homogeneous, and assume **(C)**: w(p) > w(b) for every non-A letter p with source i and every b in A. Choose c with max w(A) < c < min w(non-A from i) and put phi(i) = c, phi = 0 elsewhere. Then every kM letter L: s -> t has positive shifted weight w(L) + phi(t) - phi(s) (a positive, Adams-connected grading of kM), so every oriented cycle of the kM letter quiver has positive weight, i.e. (LF) holds. (C) is sufficient, not necessary (referee's toy kQ/(a1 c d - a2 d, a1 e), w(e) = 1). (C) holds whenever all A-arrows have the same weight, in particular for the path-length grading of a homogeneous (e.g. monomial) algebra, and whenever |A| = 1. **(LF) is not implied by positive weights alone:** kQ/(a1 c) with a1: i->1, c: 1->2, a2: i->2, w = (1, 1, 5) gives f a2^-1 of weight -3 and a negative cycle f·a2*. Reweighting w(a2) = 1 repairs it.

**Correction to E-319's (H1).** Lemmas 1-2 use only **(H1w)**: every Theta letter with a linear term has a pivot x_theta (coefficient != 0, x_theta not a Theta letter, x_theta in no other Theta letter's linear part, pivots distinct). E-319's first clause, "only Theta letters have a linear term", is never used. With (H1w) in its place the Theorem (LF)+(H1)+(H3) => kM -> N q.i. keeps its proof.

**On real parents (computed).** On all 82 LNA instances (48 of them gate-admitted J = 0 steps under E-252's reading), (LF), (H1w), [PI] (pi is a dg map, d_H^2 = 0) and (H3) all hold, and the engine gives H(I) = 0. The letter quiver of kM has no cycle at all on a line. (H1w) holds at every vertex of every LNA, by a short proof (below). Strict (H1) fails on 26 instances, every one gate-closed under E-252's reading: a non-Theta letter p has d p = (a·y) + ..., where y is a letter ending at i. On LNAs, J = 0 excludes this (proof below). **It is not excluded off the line:** the tree y: 1->2, a: 2->3, a2: 2->4, z: 3->5 with relations y a and a z is gate-admitted at 2 with J = 0, and d p = a.y is linear with p non-Theta. Its H = A is not machine-checked: it is the Anick resolution of a monomial algebra, right by Anick's theorem. There (H1w), [PI], (H3) (g(y) = g(p) = 1, g(c) = 2; leading word a2*·q for q/a2) and H(I) = 0 all hold. **Answer to the assignment:** (LF) is implied for parents with a homogeneous positive weight satisfying (C) (e.g. equal A-weights); not for every graded parent. The first clause of (H1) is not implied, and it is not needed. (H3) is checked, not implied: it is the remaining open hypothesis. "Other relations through the pivot b" are harmless: A5 220 F1 (pivot x2, relation x2x3 through it, d_H c2_3 = r1·x3) and A6 2220 F1 (three overlapping relations, pivots x2, r2, c2_5) pass.

Not claimed: (H3) for every LNA; anything about loop parents of finite-dimensional algebras beyond (LF); Hom/End(T); the R/F matching beyond E-252. **Refuted by:** a parent meeting the Proposition's hypotheses with a cycle of weight <= 0; or a model with (LF)+(H1w)+(H3) and H(I) != 0.

## Evidence

**Proof of the Proposition (vertex shift, after the referee).** Every kM letter is a.p b^-1 (prefix a when t(p) = i, suffix b when s(p) = i), with weight w(p) + w(a) - w(b), or a star b* : t(b) -> i, with weight -w(b). (i) w(p) > w_A := w(b) for b in A, for every non-A letter p from i, by induction on degree: every word of d p starts with a letter from i, which is either an A-arrow followed by a non-empty rest of positive weight, or a non-A letter from i of weight > w_A by induction. Degree-0 letters from i are all in A. This is the step that needs d p != 0 and no single-arrow word. (ii) Hence every non-star letter has positive weight: a.p and plain p trivially, and p b^-1, a.p b^-1 because w(p) > w(b). (iii) In a cycle, a star b* ends at i, and the next letter starts at i. It cannot be a star (s(b*) = t(b) != i, since there is no degree-0 loop), and it has no suffix (a suffixed letter starts at t(b) != i). So it is a.p or p with s(p) = i, of weight >= w(p) > w(b). (iii') Shift by phi(i) = c with max w(A) < c < min w(non-A from i), which exists by (C): a star b*: t(b) -> i becomes c - w(b) > 0; an unsuffixed letter from i (p or a.p, s(p) = i; it ends at t(p) or t(a), not i) becomes w(p) (+ w(a)) - c > 0; a suffixed letter p b^-1 or a.p b^-1 starts at t(b) != i and ends off i, so it keeps w(p) (+ w(a)) - w(b) > 0; letters not touching i keep their positive weight. A cycle's weight is unchanged by a potential, so it is positive. (The round-123 pairing argument is the same proof, longer.) Machine check of (iii'): `theorist_shift.py`, all 82 LNA instances, min shifted weight 1/2. **Weakest step:** "d p != 0 and no single-arrow word" is read from Construction 2.2 (each new letter kills a nonzero class, and the relations are admissible). For the 20 parents built here it holds by construction.

**(H1w) on LNAs (proof sketch).** On a line A = {a: i -> i+1}. A Theta letter is p/a, and its linear part comes only from the T-term of d(p/a) (strip a from the words a·x of d p). The base and star terms have length >= 2, because a loop at i is impossible on a line (sketch; one Anick chain per cell and degree holds for monomial LNAs, and the machine check agrees on all 82). Linear means d p contains a·x with x a single letter, which is plain, so not Theta. On a line, the cell (s, t) holds at most one Anick chain per degree, so x determines p, and the pivots are distinct and unshared. **Strict clause 1 fails => J != 0 (LNA):** the failure needs a 2-letter word y·a in some d p, with t(y) = i. Then the last relation of the chain p ends at i+1 and starts at some s <= i-1. Its part from s to i is a nonzero path killed by a, the only arrow out of i, so J_s != 0.

**Machine checks** (`theorist_lnaparent.py`, 1 s). [MM] H(parent) = A in every cell (exact), and d^2 = 0 on parent letters and on all kM letters: 0 failures on 20 parents and 82 kM's.
