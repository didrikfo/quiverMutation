## Claim

(1) Fosse's tex has no mu^+ / mu^- and no word "left" for his own mutation; his sentences fix: "right" = cocone of a minimal right approximation of P_i by the other projectives; paths composed right to left, morphisms left to right; P_i = paths starting at i; the quiver rule gives End(mu_i^R(Lambda))^op. So E-252's naming table rests on these sentences, but the identification "F = Fosse right" is the project's reading of a dimension-level match, not something the paper states about the repo's code. (2) None found among the title/abstract matches of 27 queries: a derived invariant of Nakayama algebras, not computable from the Cartan matrix, that separates same-key groups, or a statement of the Euler-form-defect (T6) kind. The closest published things are (a) 2606.15595: an enriched Hochschild "calculus with Coxeter automorphism", which for algebras of finite global dimension carries only the Coxeter matrix conjugacy class over k on HH_0 (my inference for LNAs, below), and (b) its Question `q:lattice` (Cartan-level, not non-Cartan: the invariant is the Cartan lattice, which E-232 already tests): the integral enrichment (Cartan up to Z-congruence, Phi up to GL_n(Z)-conjugacy) "for which classes is this complete?", posed as open. Not claimed: that no such invariant exists; the queries match titles and abstracts only.
