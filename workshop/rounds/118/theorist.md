## Proposed H-entry (converted: the general statement)

**proposed H-A (no number; the keeper assigns it; highest in HYPOTHESES.md is H-027, checked) -- Loop cancellation, pivot-reducible loops.** For a Construction-2.2 parent with a degree-1 loop f at i whose differential is d f = sum_p a_p r_p (a_p arrows out of i, r_p paths from t(a_p) to i, one r_p ending in an arrow b with the star letter b-bar linear in d theta_{f,a_p}), a triangular change of generators brings kM to B * T(E) with B of Lemma A shape (X1 = {w}, Y = arrows out of i, one Theta per pair), so kM -> N is a quasi-isomorphism. The existence of the triangular substitution is part of the hypothesis, NOT proved in general: M5 needed a second, product-pivot step (t_m' = t_m - e t_a, ...) to remove e bbar from d t_m; that this step exists for every model of the form (several out-arrows, longer r_p, several extra arrows) is unproved and is not claimed. Proved only for the four listed models. Falsifiable: a loop model of this form (any number of out-arrows, paths r_p of any length, d^2 = 0 holding) with H(I) != 0 in some cell (E-284 engine, `workshop/rounds/106/theorist_loopfilt.py`), or one where no choice of triangular substitution reaches the shape (then the shape is not general, or the product-pivot reduction does not always exist). Status: SUPPORTED only by M1, M2, M4, M5 (proofs, formal dg models) and the ten models of E-284 (computation); it does not bear on H-023's finite-dimensional step; the pivot-free case d f = 0 with products of several loops, M3, M3b, M7, M8 are NOT proved.
