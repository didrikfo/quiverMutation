## Proposed H-entry (converted: the general statement)

**H-030 (proposed) -- Loop cancellation.** For a Construction-2.2 parent with a degree-1 loop f at i whose differential is d f = sum_p a_p r_p (a_p arrows out of i, r_p paths from t(a_p) to i, one r_p ending in an arrow b with the star letter b-bar linear in d theta_{f,a_p}), a triangular change of generators brings kM to B * T(E) with B of Lemma A shape (X1 = {w}, Y = arrows out of i, one Theta per pair), so kM -> N is a quasi-isomorphism. Falsifiable: a loop model of this form (any number of out-arrows, paths r_p of any length, d^2 = 0 holding) with H(I) != 0 in some cell (E-284 engine, `theorist_loopfilt.py`), or one where no choice of triangular substitution reaches the shape (then the shape is not general). Status: SUPPORTED only by M1, M2, M4, M5 (proofs) and the ten models of E-284 (computation); the pivot-free case d f = 0 with products of several loops, M3, M3b, M7, M8 are NOT proved.
