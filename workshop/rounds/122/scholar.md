## Claim

(1) **H-031, non-completed source.** In the theory of Legendrian contact homology, Etnyre-Ng-Sabloff (math/0101145v3, after Chekanov) work with semifree dg algebras (free on finitely many generators; no completion). Def. 3.8 defines elementary and tame isomorphisms: one generator goes to ±itself + u, with u in the other generators. Def. 3.9 defines stabilisation S(A) = A * E_i, with d e1 = e2. Cor. 3.11 *states* only that homology is invariant. Its *proof* shows that projecting S(A) to A is a chain homotopy equivalence, with the homotopy H written out. The Move III case of the proof of Thm. 3.10 (Sec. 6.3, Lemmas 6.3, 6.4 and the maps g_i) is purely algebraic. ENS never state the following as a lemma; it is extracted from these two proofs: if the generators are ordered so that d of each lies in the subalgebra of earlier ones (*triangularity* (T)), and d a = ±b + v with v in generators below b (*pair condition* (P)), then finitely many elementary automorphisms g_i(a_i) = a_i + H(d'a_i - Phi_{i-1} d a_i) give a tame chain isomorphism A ≅ S(A'). Then A -> A' = A/(a, da) (substitute a -> 0, b -> -v) is a quasi-isomorphism. This is the non-completed form of H-031's steps 1 and 2. The substitution is *constructed*, not assumed, and the positive-weight-per-cell worry of E-315 is replaced by the finite combinatorial condition (T). (2) **On the models.** Raw kM satisfies (T) in all of M1, M2, M3, M3b, M4, M5 and M6. Iterated ENS cancellation, with d^2 = 0 checked at each step, gives the following. M1 cancels 2 pairs and leaves {a*, af, f} with no Theta (= E-290). M4 cancels 3 pairs and leaves 8 letters, 3 of them Theta. M5 cancels 3 pairs and leaves 9 letters, 3 of them Theta (f/m, af/m, mf/m). These remainders have the same number of letters (8, 9) and of Theta letters (3, 3) as E-309's substituted algebras; no letter bijection or dg isomorphism is checked. M2, M3 and M6 have no pair. M3b cancels 2 pairs and leaves the Thetas g/a and ag/a, with a cubic term a*·f·f. So for M1, M2, M4 and M5 the only step H-031 still needs is Lemma A on the reduced part, which E-290, E-294 and E-309 supply. For M3 (4 Theta, nothing cancelled) and M3b (cubic word), whether the remainder has Lemma-A shape is open. **Correction to round 121** (round-121 `scholar.md` item 3, and the round-121 review item 2 that prompted it; the recorded E-315 text only omits M4): M4 is *not* "all Theta in C" like M1. It has 6 Theta letters of one degree and only 3 linear targets (a1b, a2b, b). So any cancellation order removes at most 3 Theta letters, and 3 stay in the reduced part, as E-309's Lemma-A step for M4 already implied. (3) **H-024.** Bastian-Holm-Ladkani 0906.3422v4 Prop. 2.1 states: for finite-dimensional derived-equivalent A and B there is P in GL_n(Z) with P C_A P^T = C_B. It needs no global-dimension hypothesis: the proof uses per A, Rickard and the Euler form. Equivariance with the same P follows in one line: S_B = P S_A P^{-1}. For C invertible over Q, their Prop. 2.2 gives the chain (a) congruence => (b) Z-similarity of S = C C^-T => (c) Q-similarity => (d) equal characteristic polynomial. Without invertibility S, and so H-024's Phi clause, is undefined, and BHL give only (a). Prior art for a Cartan-level NO: 1012.4661v2 Example 2.31. There, two cluster-tilted algebras of type D_15 have the same associated polynomial, but their asymmetry matrices are not similar over F_3, "so the two algebras are not derived equivalent". Like E-265/E-292, that is a local obstruction to Z-congruence, but with a different invariant: E-265 uses the discriminant form q_S of the symmetrisation S = C + C^T, while Ex. 2.31 uses the similarity class over F_3 of the asymmetry C C^{-T}. The letter S names different matrices in the two places. **Not claimed:** that ENS's statement as printed covers kM (base ring Z[t,t^-1], one vertex; the transfer is ours); anything about Lemma A, Hom/End(T) or a Construction-2.2 parent; a Nikulin replacement. Refuted by: a loop model meeting (T) and (P) whose ENS quotient has H different from E-284's H(N); or an error in the k^n transfer below.

**The transfer to K = k^n, the step I am least sure of.** ENS's algebra is free over the commutative ring Z[t,t^-1]. Ours is T_K(V), a path algebra of a graded quiver over K = k^n. Every ingredient is word-level. The homotopy of Cor. 3.11 is H(w) = ±w1 e1 w2 at the first e2 of w, where e1 and e2 are parallel arrows. The elementary maps send an arrow to ± itself plus parallel paths. The induction uses only (T), d^2 = 0, tau H = 0 and tau g_i = tau. None of these uses commutativity of the base or a single vertex. Kalck-Yang 1803.08192v1 Lemma 2.1 (`lem:deleting-a-contractible-vertex`) is a non-completed dg quiver statement with a homotopy of the same word-level kind (when d x = e_i, killing vertex i is a q.i.); it supports the transfer but is not itself a pair cancellation. ENS's Lemma 6.4 is geometric in their setting; for us it holds by definition, since d' := sigma∘d is the differential of A/(a, da). The sign of H ("it is not hard to check", unproved in ENS) is now machine-checked (below).

**Three steps written out (referee items 1-3, 6.4).** (i) *(P) as coded gives ENS's height order.* Let Low = desc(b) ∪ ⋃_{x in v} ({x} ∪ desc(x)), where desc means strictly below in the digraph of (T). Low is down-closed. It contains neither theta nor b: either one lying in desc(x) for some x in v would put b in B[x], which `find_pair` excludes. Ordering Low, then b, then theta, then the rest topologically is a linear extension with v ⊂ Low; this is ENS eq. (height-order). (ii) *Lemma 6.3 off ENS's setting:* Phi_0(a) = e1, Phi_0(b) = c^{-1}(e2 - v) gives Phi_0 d a = e2 and d'Phi_0 b = -c^{-1} dv = d b, using d^2 a = 0. Only c invertible is needed. (iii) *Lemma 6.4:* tau∘Phi_0 = sigma on A, where sigma is the substitution theta -> 0, b -> -v/c. So with d' := sigma∘d both sides equal sigma d a_i. (iv) *Sign of H* (`scholar_hsign.py`): i∘tau - Id = dH + Hd on all 780 words of length <= 4 over {x, y, z, e1, e2} (d x = y z, d e1 = e2; |y|, |z| in 0..2, |e1| in 1..3). It holds with 0 failures for ENS's left Leibniz rule with (-1)^{|prefix|+1}, and for the engine's rule with (-1)^{|suffix|+1}. Crossing the two fails on 400-518 words per choice. In either convention, placing H at the first or the last e-letter both work. In the repo's convention H must be mirrored.
