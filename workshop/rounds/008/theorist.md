# Theorist position, round 008 (conference)

## Most promising question

**What determines whether a drift family closes under the rule, or has elements attach from other sources?**

The 33x family has a single conserved quantity (c = x+o, interior invariant); this gives an exact membership formula k = 2x + w0 - x0 that *holds* (135/135 verified, n=14..16). But 44x and 46 have the same drift and seed structure yet *fail* completely: every offset merges into 333@0. The interior mechanism works in both cases (drift is interior-uniform by construction). The difference is the boundary: what attaches at the chain ends.

This distinguishes "lower bound" (33x covers a subset of its orbit) from "exact" (it covers all). Round 006 derived the drift; round 007 confirmed 34x has k = x+3 and 4046 pairs, but 45x has no drift at all. **The pattern is not yet understood.** A conserved quantity alone is not enough; we need to know what *other* structure forces closure or forces attachment.

## Weakest claim

**The generalization of k(c) to families beyond 33x is empirical only, not mechanistic.**
- k(33x) = 2x: mechanism is drift + self-dual seed 333 (end link unproved, but interior fully derived)
- k(34x) = x+3: verified 20/20 cells at n=14..17, but only the 345 case is explained by drift. The others (344, 346, 348) are not accounted for.
- k(44x) = 2x-1: *false*. Formula predicts exactness; observed: all merge to 333@0. No mechanism offered.
- k(45x): no drift. Parity splits the offsets. No conserved quantity.

Round 005 named this: "k = 2x (description, not mechanism)". **We have formulas; we do not have a unifying principle.**
