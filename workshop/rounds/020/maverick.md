# Maverick round 020

## Most promising question

**Are monomial cords derived equivalent to LNA algebras?**

The Coxeter polynomial is provably blind to (cords, relations), yet it sees derived invariants in other contexts. If a monomial cord is the derived equivalent of an LNA—making it invisible to Coxeter via a derived bridge—this would explain both the null MONO search at n = 8 and why the candidates E-078 differ in kind from sum-cord controls. The test case is Lambda(1,3,m), which is derived-discrete; monomial cords could be invisible in the same way. This moves the H-017 question from "does a monomial cord exist" to "does one exist in a different derived class."

## Weakest claim

No positive MONO control at n = 8. 

This rests on 6 LNAs walked to depth ≤ 5; the full census has 429. The absence of monomial cords in a small sample of one LNA class does not prove they do not exist globally—only that they don't cluster near those six. E-094/E-089 conflate "none found" with "none exist"; the search was not exhaustive.
