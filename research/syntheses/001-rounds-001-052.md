# Synthesis 1: the research workshop, rounds 001-052

*2026-10-09 · checkpoint before the first merge of `workshop` into `main`.
Written by the session that runs the workshop, from `research/` (E-055 to
E-165), the proceedings and the digest. Every claim here points to an entry;
the entry, not this page, is the record. Full round transcripts (submissions,
reviews, notebooks, raw outputs) stay on the `workshop` branch.*

## In one paragraph

Fifty-two rounds of a six-persona workshop (39 ordinary rounds, 13
conferences) added 111 experiment entries (E-055 to E-165). Most of the effort went into one question about
the tool rather than the mathematics: **does the guarded mutation walk stay
inside one derived class?** The answer is now well mapped. The gate alone is
unsound; the Coxeter-key guard catches almost all of the gate's mistakes but
not all (25 of about 160 000 key-keeping steps at n = 7 fail the tilting
test); the failing steps are exactly those with a non-zero `J_i`, which has a
clean description; and all 25 of those children are still in the right class
by other, tilting-only paths, provided a step that passes the tilting test is a
derived equivalence (the "J = 0 premise", which rests on two papers nobody
could read from here). No recorded merge examined depends on a bad
step. On the mathematics proper, the workshop sharpened H-021 (the "mirror"
clause is false on every reading; the centre formula survives), derived the
`33x`/`aax` families, kept H-017 alive but showed its proposed invariant
cannot decide it, and answered the vertex-deletion question (S-1) in the
negative with a precise threshold law. No hypothesis was settled.

## 1. The walk's soundness (threads T5, T10; H-015)

**What is established**

- The gate (`mutationIsPossibleAtVertex`) admits non-tilting steps that are
  reachable from LNAs: n = 6 at distance 8, n = 7..9 at distance 5-7
  (E-086). Earlier "none" results were depth-limited (E-059 annotated).
- Characterisation: at a gate-admitted vertex the step is silting but not
  tilting exactly when some `J_i = Hom(S_v, e_i A) != 0` (E-124, E-128,
  E-130; conditional on Aihara-Iyama Thm 2.31 *[round 055, E-171: Thm 2.31 and
  Thm 2.32(b) checked against the LaTeX; the iff is 2.31 + 2.32(b)]*).
  `dim J_i <= d_i - 1` with `d_i = dim e_i A e_v` (E-128).
- Cartan defect of such a step: `C_B = r C_A r^T + H`, `H_{vi} = dim J_i`,
  all other entries 0 (E-138; 28 000 steps, 0 failures). The library's
  Cartan-congruence check is `tiltingPlus` read through the rewrite (E-095,
  E-099).
- Mechanism on walks: a two-term kernel element (rule W, E-109, E-113;
  derivation and limits E-112, E-116, E-122, E-123). Out-degree 3-4 cases
  are real (E-129, E-133, E-137).
- The Coxeter-key guard refused every non-tilting step in the walks of
  E-086 and E-096 (n = 8 class 2 to depth 8, E-155: 0 of 104 629). But it is
  **not** a tilting test: at n = 7 classes 1, 2 there are key-keeping,
  gate-admitted steps that fail `tiltingPlus` (E-147; 16 of 80 978 and 9 of
  79 143, first at parent depth 7-8, E-151).
- Those 25 children are, conditionally, still in their class: all 25 are
  joined to an LNA of the class (or its dual) by printed paths of J = 0
  tilting steps of length 6-13 (E-157, E-160, E-163); an independent
  Hom(T, T[m]) test, written without the library's tilting code, accepts
  every path edge it was run on and rejects the 25 bad steps (E-161, E-163).
- The recorded merges examined do not depend on a J != 0 step: the 10 F-041
  merges at n = 8 have witnesses of length 6 whose 60 edges all pass the
  gate, `tiltingPlus` and the key (E-150, E-156); F-037 (one of the two
  n = 10 merges) has J = 0 paths; the other (group A) is not yet replayed
  (E-164).
- Adding a J = 0 / `tiltingPlus` check to the guard costs 8-11 % per step
  (E-151).

**Library changes made by the workshop** (need review before `main`)

- `arrowPaths.reduceAgainstPivots` was not a normal form; fixed with a unit
  test that fails on the old code (E-087, E-091, round 017). Earlier counts
  re-run and unchanged where re-runnable (E-092, E-096, E-104).
- An opt-in Cartan-congruence check in `procedure.py` (E-104).
- `longSquare` fixed for parallel arrows (E-111).
- `batch.py orbits` task (round 003); `merges.py --witness` (E-156).
- Known wrong docstrings, not yet changed: `search.mutationSearchDepthFirst`
  says the Coxeter guard keeps the walk in one derived class; that is
  unsupported as a tilting claim (E-147, E-151). `canonicalKey` returns
  `None` on parallel-arrow bundles, contrary to its docstring (E-160).

**Laws that did not survive** (each found on a capped sample, then broken):
"d_i = 2 whenever J_i != 0" (E-131, broken by E-133, E-134); "out(i) = 3"
(E-137, E-140); "J != 0 moves the key" (E-140, E-142, broken by E-143,
E-147); the orbit relation `e_i = F^s e_w` (E-145, broken by E-147, E-148);
"the n = 6 fans meet an LNA" (E-136, unsupported by E-139); "the fans reach
no LNA" (E-134, a bounded miss, E-136).

**Open:** the premise itself. That a J = 0 step passing `tiltingPlus` is a
derived equivalence rests on Aihara-Iyama 2.31 / 2.32(b) (read, E-171) and Ladkani 2.3(c), and the independent test is Cartan-level with generation assumed (generation proved per loopless step, E-168); the
round-052 conference named it the weakest claim the workshop relies on, and
asked whether the condition is vertex-level or whole-T. No derived invariant
with a power control exists to show a child is *outside* a class (E-154:
Cartan invariants cannot; E-159 definition). The second n = 10 merge (group
A) has no witness path yet (E-164).

## 2. Cores and offsets (threads T1-T4; H-020, H-021)

**Established**

- H-021's "mirror" clause fails as an "exactly when" under the literal, the
  strict and a third reading, over all 139 cores at n = 13 (E-055, E-058).
  What survives is the centre `s = n - k(c)` for cores that pair.
- For interior outside blocks the centre is the first plus the last outside
  offset (13/13 at n = 13, 3/3 at 14; E-062), and it survives a shuffle null
  (E-069, which also shows 39 of 109 fits were vacuous).
- Families: `k(33x) = 2x`, `d = x - 3`, derived from a drift of the double
  mutation with lower bound proved (E-063, E-067); `aax` closes into chain
  pairs with `k = 2x + 3 - a` for a = 3, 5, 6 (7 at n = 15), `44x` joins the
  big orbit (E-073); `34x` pairs as `o <-> hi - o`, `k = x + 3` (E-070).
- Orbit-plus-mirror classes refine the Coxeter-key classes in all 139 cores
  at n = 10, 12..16 (E-066, E-072). The key-coarser cores are the same 9
  words at even n and 10 at odd n (E-076), and they alternate between two
  single-relation orbits P, Q (E-079, E-082, E-085); no invariant separating
  P from Q was found.
- The `444` orbit: the 3-letter merged words lie in one big orbit per n
  (E-081, E-088); `3334`, `2455` are one double mutation (lemma R) from `35`
  and lie in the class of `333@1` (E-090); "exactly one placement in the
  `444` orbit" is not enriched over a null (E-093, E-098, E-100), so "letter
  4 is special" is not supported (E-077, E-081).
- H-020's floating rules of window width <= 5 hold at every length w + 5 to
  11 (0 failures in 11 170 applications; E-159); widths 6-8 hold at one
  length each (0 failures in 43 116; E-165). Removing the table changes no
  verdict in the reduced walk, which is already the double mutation of F-032,
  so H-020 may be a statement about the move set rather than the table
  (E-165, round-052 ledger).

**Open:** H-021 and H-020 as theorems; the 7 cores that pair at even n only
(E-061); what separates P from Q; `44x`, `45x` (no drift).

## 3. Cords and relations (thread T6; H-017)

- Not refuted at n = 9 to depth 6: all 16 K = 4 candidates and the K = 1
  candidates reach no quipu-class member (E-074, E-075, E-078, E-083).
- The search has power where tested: controls find their source at exactly
  their depth and never one step short (E-071, E-074, E-084, E-089).
- Cord members carry sum relations; no monomial cord member at n = 4, 5, 8
  (E-091, E-094). A cord-member criterion: a relation of >= 3 arrows that is
  not blocked gives depth 1; a mirror-chain rule predicts depth with 0
  mismatches at n = 8..10 and 13 of 13 out-of-sample (E-101, E-103, E-106).
- The proposed invariant (Euler signature) is a function of the Cartan
  matrix, so it cannot see (cords, relations) and cannot decide H-017; as a
  separator from quipu polynomials it is new on 16 LNAs at n = 11 (E-065,
  E-162).

**Open:** a positive monomial control at n = 8; any test of H-017 that can
fail.

## 4. Vertex deletion (S-1, the human's optional question)

- Deleting a vertex keeps same-class pairs together about twice as often as
  chance, for no choice of position (E-114).
- Stripping a free end of K >= 3 vertices is a function of the class at
  n = 8, 9, 10, fails at n = 11 (E-120). The image is a function of
  (K, core word) (E-127, E-135); first failures are at K0 = 3, 4 at
  n = 11, 13, and K0 = 5 at n = 15 is predicted, not run (E-141, E-146).
- The "room to move" scenario (a long-length pattern carried down by
  deletion to an uncovered shorter class) was not found; deletion does not
  transport classes in general, and the threshold law says when it does.

## 7. Takeaways

**For the project**

1. The result that matters most beyond the workshop is that the Coxeter-key
   guard is not a tilting test. Make the tilting check (J = 0 or
   `tiltingPlus`, about 10 % per step) available in the search, and reword
   the `mutationSearchDepthFirst` docstring. Recorded merges examined so far
   are unaffected.
2. Some results do not depend on the premise and look solid enough to be
   findings: the centre formula for interior cores (E-062, E-069), the `33x`
   family (E-067), the vertex-deletion threshold law (E-120, E-127, E-135,
   E-141, E-146), and, conditional only on AI 2.31, the `J_i` criterion and
   Cartan defect (E-124, E-128, E-130, E-138). The ledgers so far have only
   looked at hypotheses touched in the last four rounds; a ledger pass over
   the older threads should consider writing these as F-entries.
3. Two citations carry weight: Aihara-Iyama arXiv:1009.3370 (Thm 2.31/2.32)
   and CHZ arXiv:2509.12983 (Cor 3.6). *[Round 055, E-171: both read from
   the LaTeX; the numbers stand; Cor 3.6 states no "monomial" hypothesis and
   is an iff only for monomial `I`.]*

**For the method**

1. Laws found on capped samples or on class 0 alone failed repeatedly within
   one to three rounds. Scope in the title, and an independent rebuild of
   the main claim, were the habits that caught them (E-139, E-147, E-161).
2. Review was real but toothless until round 045. The same-round response
   (ROUND.md step 3.5) raised round cost from about $3-6 to $5-8.50 and
   length to 80-130 minutes, and replaced "accepted with qualifications" by
   answered points and narrowed claims.
3. The workshop drifted onto one thread (tool soundness) for 20+ rounds. It
   was a valuable thread, but the breadth slot (round 045 on) is what brought
   H-010, H-017, H-020 back.
