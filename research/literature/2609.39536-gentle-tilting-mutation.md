# Tilting mutation of gentle algebras

**arXiv:** [2609.39536](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39536) (v2) · Deng, Geng, Liu

Read in round 103 (experimentalist, LaTeX, theorem numbers by LaTeX counter; PDF unchecked).

- **Thm 4.2(3):** the mutation at k exists iff for every outgoing arrow beta at k there is an incoming arrow alpha with alpha beta not in I (loops included).
- **Def. 7.1** defines mu_k^+; **Thm 7.2:** the result is again gentle (Thm "7.1" in the first draft of E-280 was a definition).
- Compared with the library gate on all 3 168 gentle LNA cells, n = 3..9: the criterion equals the repo LEFT gate, its mirror the RIGHT gate, no disagreement (E-280).
- Covers no non-gentle LNA (no relation of length >= 3), says nothing about Nakayama derived classes.
- Also noted in round 103: arXiv:2602.04571 (Calvo Cortes-Frost), Prop. 2.3: ideals of kA_n without idempotents <-> Dyck paths, C_n of them (LNAs are C_{n-1}).
