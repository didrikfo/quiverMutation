# Derived equivalences from mutations of quivers with potential

**arXiv:** [0906.0761](https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.0761) (v3 read) · B. Keller, D. Yang · Adv. Math. 226 (2011), no. 3, 2118-2168 (as printed in Oppermann's bibliography, 1504.02617v1)

*Read in LaTeX by the scholar, workshop round 121 (E-315).*

Theorem 2.3 restates DWZ 4.4-4.6 (splitting) for loop-free quivers. **Lemma 2.10**: for the *complete Ginzburg* dg algebra of a *direct sum* (Q', W') + (Q'', W'') with (Q'', W'') trivial, the projection to Gamma(Q', W') is a quasi-isomorphism. Proof: filter the kernel by word length; the associated graded is (U+V)^i / U^i with V contractible; Mittag-Leffler (Lemma 7.4) finishes. Lemma 2.9: right-equivalent QPs have isomorphic Ginzburg algebras.

**For us:** it does not cover H-031 (kM is not Ginzburg, not completed, and on raw generators the trivial letters are not a direct summand; even the ideal condition its proof needs fails: E-315). It is the model for the trivial-part-removal step.
