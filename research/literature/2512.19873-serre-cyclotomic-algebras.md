- Def `def:serre_cyclotomic` (tex l. 544): A is (l,m,n)-Serre cyclotomic if Iwanaga-Gorenstein and the inverse dualizing bimodule admits f_1..f_l in Hom(A, N^n[m]) with Cone(f_l) (x) ... (x) Cone(f_1) = 0.
- (1,m,n)-Serre cyclotomic = fractionally Calabi-Yau of dimension (m,n) (l. 548-550). Order l gives (Phi^{2n} - 1)^l = 0 and polynomial entropy at most l - 1.
- "Cyclotomic" there means some non-zero power of Phi is unipotent (l. 142-143); periodic means finite order.
- Graded-gentle classification (`prop:graded_gentle`, l. 722): types A and A~ only; silent on Nakayama.
